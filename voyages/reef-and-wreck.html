The voyage

Two dives, one wreck, and a boat that waits where you surface.

Built around the divers aboard rather than a fixed schedule. Tanks, weights and a dive guide travel with you; the sites are chosen on the day for current and light.

The day

How it runs

Timings are indicative. The captain sets the final route on the morning, for tide, wind and light.

08:00
Departure — Kit set up underway.
09:30
First dive — A channel or thila, chosen for the tide.
12:00
Surface interval — Lunch at anchor and a long rest.
14:00
Second dive — Wreck or reef, depending on conditions.
17:00
Alongside — Rinse down at the marina.
