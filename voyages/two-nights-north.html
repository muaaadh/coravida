The day

How it runs

Timings are indicative. The captain sets the final route on the morning, for tide, wind and light.

Day one
Board at midday, cross north, and anchor for the night off an uninhabited island.
Day two
Dawn snorkel, a long lunch, and a second anchorage further out.
Day three
Breakfast underway and alongside Hulhumalé by noon.
