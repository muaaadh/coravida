The day

How it runs

Timings are indicative. The captain sets the final route on the morning, for tide, wind and light.

Day one
North — Board at midday, cross north, and anchor for the night off an uninhabited island.
Day two
Further out — Dawn snorkel, a long lunch, and a second anchorage.
Day three
Home — Breakfast underway and alongside Hulhumalé by noon.
