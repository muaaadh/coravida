The day

How it runs

Timings are indicative. The captain sets the final route on the morning, for tide, wind and light.

09:00
Hulhumalé Marina — Boarding, briefing, and a slow run out past the harbour wall.
10:30
First reef — Snorkelling on a sheltered thila, gear and guide aboard.
13:00
Lunch at anchor — Maldivian table served in the cockpit while the boat swings on the tide.
15:30
Sandbank — An hour of dry sand and shallow water, weather permitting.
17:00
Return — Back alongside for the evening.
