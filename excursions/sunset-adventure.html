Shark Point, a sandbank, and the sun going down on the way home.

The day

How it runs

Timings are indicative. The captain sets the final route on the morning, for tide, wind and light.

  1. 13:30
    Hulhumalé jetty

    South towards the atoll rim.

  2. 14:15
    Shark Point

    Snorkelling on the reef, among the sharks and everything else it holds.

  3. 15:30
    Sandbank

    White sand, a clear lagoon, and the best hour for photographs.

  4. 16:30
    Evening snack

    A light snack and refreshments aboard.

  5. 17:00
    Sunset cruise

    North again, slowly, with the sun going down.

  6. 18:00
    Hulhumalé jetty

    Alongside.
