The day

How it runs

Timings are indicative. The captain sets the final route on the morning, for tide, wind and light.

  1. 09:00
    Hulhumalé jetty

    South across the atoll.

  2. 10:00
    Shark Point, Embudu

    Snorkelling alongside reef sharks on the village reef.

  3. 11:15
    Coral garden

    The reef off Taj — coral and tropical fish in shallow water.

  4. 12:15
    Sandbank

    White sand and a clear lagoon. Swimming and photographs.

  5. 13:00
    Gulhi

    Ashore on a local island.

  6. 13:30
    Lunch

    Lunch, and time to sit.

  7. 14:30
    Coral life

    One more snorkelling stop on the way back north.

  8. 16:30
    Sunset cruise

    Back towards Hulhumalé with the light behind.

  9. 18:00
    Hulhumalé jetty

    Alongside.
