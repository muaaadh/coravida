Aboard

What the day can hold

Six things the vessel is set up for. Take one, or take all of them if the tide agrees.

01

Diving

Two tanks aboard, a guide who reads the current, and sites chosen on the day.

  • Two tanks and weights per diver
  • Guide aboard, sites chosen on the day
  • Nitrox on request

02

Snorkelling

Shallow thilas and reef edges where the water stays clear all afternoon.

  • Masks, fins and vests in every size
  • A guide in the water with you
  • Two or three stops in a full day

03

Sandbanks

A table, an umbrella, and an hour of dry sand that will not be there tomorrow.

  • Low water only — the tide sets the hour
  • Umbrella, mats and a cold box carried across
  • Everything comes back to the boat

04

Sunset

Out at four, canapés on the flybridge, home under the last of the light.

  • Five hours, four until nine
  • Canapés and sparkling wine aboard
  • The southern rim, weather permitting

05

Fishing

Evening handline off the reef, or big game trolling on the outer edge.

  • Handlines, rods and bait aboard
  • Trolling on the outer edge at first light
  • The galley cooks what you keep

06

Celebrations

Proposals, birthdays and small weddings, set on deck or on the sand.

  • Deck or sandbank, dressed before you board
  • Cake, flowers and a photographer arranged
  • Twelve aboard for the day
